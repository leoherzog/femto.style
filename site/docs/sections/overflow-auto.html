Overflow auto

.overflow-auto scrolls content that overflows it, such as a wide table.

A scroll container with nothing focusable inside needs tabindex="0", so that keyboard users can scroll it, and a role and a name, such as role="region" and an aria-label, so that screen readers announce it.

<div class="overflow-auto" tabindex="0" role="region" aria-label="Planets">
  <table>
    ...
  </table>
</div>

Used in the Checkout and Dashboard examples.
