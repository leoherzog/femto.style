Radios

<input type="radio"> is drawn as a circle with a thick ring in the primary color when checked.

Put each radio inside its <label>. aria-disabled="true" on the label of a disabled radio dims the label too.

Language preference: 
<fieldset>
  <legend>Language preference:</legend>
  <label>
    <input type="radio" name="language" checked>
    English
  </label>
  <label>
    <input type="radio" name="language">
    French
  </label>
  <label>
    <input type="radio" name="language">
    Mandarin
  </label>
  <label>
    <input type="radio" name="language">
    Thai
  </label>
  <label aria-disabled="true">
    <input type="radio" name="language" disabled>
    Dothraki
  </label>
</fieldset>

Horizontal stacking

Radios followed by their <label for>, rather than inside it, sit side by side on one line.

Second language: 
<fieldset>
  <legend>Second language:</legend>
  <input type="radio" id="hindi" name="second-language" checked>
  <label for="hindi">Hindi</label>
  <input type="radio" id="swahili" name="second-language">
  <label for="swahili">Swahili</label>
  <input type="radio" id="navi" name="second-language" disabled>
  <label for="navi" aria-disabled="true">Na'vi</label>
</fieldset>

Validation states

aria-invalid="true" or "false" gives a radio the invalid or valid border color.

Validation state: 
<fieldset>
  <legend>Validation state:</legend>
  <label>
    <input type="radio" name="validation-states" aria-invalid="false">
    Valid
  </label>

  <label>
    <input type="radio" name="validation-states" aria-invalid="true">
    Invalid
  </label>
</fieldset>

Used in the Contact form, Checkout, Settings, and Search results examples.

forms/checkbox-radio-switch.css styles it: Checkboxes names the tokens it reads.
