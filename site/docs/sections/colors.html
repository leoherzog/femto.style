Customization

Colors

femto.colors.css holds the palette of femto's 20 theme colors, in 19 shades each, as custom properties and utility classes.

Choose a color or a shade to see its values and names.

On 31 shades, such as azure-450, the .femto-background-* class sets white text with a contrast below 4.5:1. Their swatches and the color dialog show black text instead.

Usage

femto.colors.css is a separate, standalone file that works with or without femto. Link it in the <head> of your page:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@leoherzog/femto@3/dist/femto.colors.min.css">

Use dist/femto.colors.min.css (about 78 KB, 7 KB gzipped and under 5 KB with Brotli), dist/femto.colors.css, or the source, css/femto.colors.css.

.femto-color-* sets the text color, and .femto-background-* sets the background color and a white or black text color:

Jade heading

A card on a pumpkin background
<h2 class="femto-color-jade-500">Jade heading</h2>
<article class="femto-background-pumpkin-300">
  A card on a pumpkin background
</article>

In your own CSS, use the custom properties:

h2 {
  color: var(--femto-color-jade-500);
}

Names

Name What it is
--femto-color-<color>-<shade> A palette color: one of the 20 theme colors in a shade from 50 to 950, such as --femto-color-jade-550
.femto-color-<color>-<shade> Sets the text color
.femto-background-<color>-<shade> Sets the background color and a white or black text color
--femto-color-<color>, .femto-color-<color>, .femto-background-<color> The unshaded names for one of the color's shades, such as --femto-color-jade for jade-550. The large swatch at the top of each color above shows that shade.
--femto-color-light, --femto-color-dark The white and black text colors the background classes use
