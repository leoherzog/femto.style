Forms

Textarea

<textarea> has the style of the other fields, and the visitor can resize it vertically.

<textarea
  name="bio"
  placeholder="Write a professional short bio..."
  aria-label="Professional short bio"
>
</textarea>

Disabled textarea

<textarea name="disabled" aria-label="Disabled textarea" disabled>
  Disabled
</textarea>

Read-only textarea

<textarea name="read-only" aria-label="Read-only textarea" readonly>
  Read-only
</textarea>

Validation states

aria-invalid="true" or "false" gives a textarea the invalid or valid border color, and an icon at the top right.

<textarea name="valid" aria-label="Valid" aria-invalid="false">
  Valid
</textarea>

<textarea name="invalid" aria-label="Invalid" aria-invalid="true">
  Invalid
</textarea>

A <small> right after the textarea takes the matching color.

Looks good! Please provide a valid value! 
<textarea
  name="valid"
  aria-label="Valid"
  aria-invalid="false"
  aria-describedby="valid-helper"
>
  Valid
</textarea>
<small id="valid-helper">Looks good!</small>

<textarea
  name="invalid"
  aria-label="Invalid"
  aria-invalid="true"
  aria-describedby="invalid-helper"
>
  Invalid
</textarea>
<small id="invalid-helper">
  Please provide a valid value!
</small>
