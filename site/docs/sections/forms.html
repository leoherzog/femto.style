Forms

Forms overview

Form fields are styled from their elements alone, and text inputs, selects and textareas fill the width of their container.

Text inputs and drop-down selects are as tall as a button, so they line up with the buttons in a form. A textarea is taller, and so is a select with multiple or a size above 1. Checkboxes, radios, switches and range inputs have sizes of their own.

<form>
  <label>
    First name
    <input
      type="text"
      name="first_name"
      placeholder="First name"
      autocomplete="given-name"
    >
  </label>
  <label>
    Email
    <input
      type="email"
      name="email"
      placeholder="Email"
      autocomplete="email"
    >
  </label>

  <input
    type="submit"
    value="Subscribe"
  >
</form>

A field can be inside its <label>, or after a label that names it with for.

<!-- Input inside label -->
<label>
  First name
  <input
    type="text"
    name="first_name"
    placeholder="First name"
    autocomplete="given-name"
  >
</label>

<!-- Input after label -->
<label for="email">Email</label>
<input
  type="email"
  id="email"
  placeholder="Email"
  autocomplete="email"
>

Helper text

A <small> right after a field is its helper text, in the muted color. With aria-invalid on the field, it takes the valid or invalid color: see Input.

We’ll never share your email with anyone else. 
<input
  type="email"
  name="email"
  placeholder="Email"
  autocomplete="email"
  aria-label="Email"
  aria-describedby="email-helper"
>
<small id="email-helper">
  We’ll never share your email with anyone else.
</small>

Usage with grid

Fields in a .grid sit side by side from 768px and stack on smaller screens. See Grid.

<form>
  <div class="grid">
    <input
      type="text"
      name="login"
      placeholder="Login"
      aria-label="Login"
      autocomplete="username"
    >
    <input
      type="password"
      name="password"
      placeholder="Password"
      aria-label="Password"
      autocomplete="current-password"
    >
    <input
      type="submit"
      value="Log in"
    >
  </div>
</form>

Usage with group

To join fields and buttons in one row, use role="group".

Used in many examples, such as the Sign up, Contact form and Checkout.
