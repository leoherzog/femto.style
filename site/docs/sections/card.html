Components

Card

An <article> is a card: a padded block with rounded corners, a background and a shadow.

I’m a card!
<article>I’m a card!</article>

Header and footer

A <header> or <footer> directly inside a card reaches its edges, with a background of its own and a border toward the body.

Header
Body
Footer
<article>
  <header>Header</header>
  Body
  <footer>Footer</footer>
</article>

Spacing

The examples above have femto’s default spacing. css/options/responsive-spacings.css gives cards more spacing at each breakpoint: see Options.
