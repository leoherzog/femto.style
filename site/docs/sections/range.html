Forms

Range

<input type="range"> is a full-width slider with a thin track and a round thumb.

The track and thumb take their active colors while the slider is pressed or has focus.

<label>
  Brightness
  <input type="range">
</label>

<label>
  Contrast
  <input type="range" value="40">
</label>

Used in the Every element example.

forms/input-range.css reads --femto-border-radius, --femto-range-active-border-color, --femto-range-border-color, --femto-range-thumb-active-color, --femto-range-thumb-border-color, --femto-range-thumb-color, and --femto-transition. See Tokens.
