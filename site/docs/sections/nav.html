Components

Nav

<nav> lays out its lists in one row, with their links, buttons and form fields lined up.

  • Acme Corp
  • About
  • Services
  • Products
<nav>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Acme Corp</strong></li>
  </ul>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="#">About</a></li>
    <li><a href="#">Services</a></li>
    <li><a href="#">Products</a></li>
  </ul>
</nav>

The nav spreads its <ul> lists across its width. Their items sit side by side with no markers, and their links are underlined only on hover.

Breadcrumb

<nav aria-label="breadcrumb"> is a breadcrumb, with a divider between its items. Write the current page as plain text, or as a link with aria-current="page", which femto shows as plain text.

  • Home
  • Services
  • Design
<nav aria-label="breadcrumb">
  <ul>
    <li><a href="#">Home</a></li>
    <li><a href="#">Services</a></li>
    <li>Design</li>
  </ul>
</nav>

Set --femto-nav-breadcrumb-divider on the nav to change the divider.

  • Home
  • Services
  • Design
<nav
  aria-label="breadcrumb"
  style="--femto-nav-breadcrumb-divider: '✨';"
>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="#">Home</a></li>
    <li><a href="#">Services</a></li>
    <li>Design</li>
  </ul>
</nav>

Overflow

Nav links have padding, so their keyboard focus ring has room, and negative margins that keep their text lined up with the content around the nav. The nav keeps overflow: visible, so the ring is not cut off. Below, the dashed lines are the edges of two navs, and the second shows the focus rings.
