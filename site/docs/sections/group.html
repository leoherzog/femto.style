Components

Group

role="group" joins buttons, inputs and selects in one row, and role="search" also rounds its ends.

A group is as wide as its container. Its children share their borders, and only the outer corners are rounded. Unlike .grid, a group stays in one row on small screens, so keep its labels short.

<form>
  <div role="group">
    <input name="email" type="email" placeholder="Enter your email" autocomplete="email">
    <input type="submit" value="Subscribe">
  </div>
</form>

When a child has focus, the group draws one focus ring around itself, in the style of an input or of a button, depending on which child has focus.

<form>
  <div role="group">
    <input name="email" type="email" placeholder="Email" autocomplete="email">
    <input name="password" type="password" placeholder="Password">
    <input type="submit" value="Log in">
  </div>
</form>

Search

role="search" joins its children the same way and rounds the ends, like a search input.

<form role="search">
  <input name="search" type="search" placeholder="Search" aria-label="Search">
  <input type="submit" value="Search">
</form>

Buttons

role="group" also joins a row of buttons.

<div role="group">
  <button type="button">Day</button>
  <button type="button">Week</button>
</div>

aria-current shows a button as current, with its hover colors. Here the current button is Week.

<div role="group">
  <button type="button">Day</button>
  <button type="button" aria-current="true">Week</button>
</div>

Each button keeps its own style.

<div role="group">
  <button type="button" class="secondary">Day</button>
  <button type="button" class="contrast">Week</button>
</div>
