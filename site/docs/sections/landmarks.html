Layout

Landmarks and sections

The page's header, main and footer, and every section, get vertical spacing.

<header>, <main> and <footer> as direct children of <body> get a vertical padding of --femto-block-spacing-vertical, which is --femto-spacing, 1rem, by default.

<body>
  <header>...</header>
  <main>...</main>
  <footer>...</footer>
</body>

Section

Every <section> gets a bottom margin of --femto-block-spacing-vertical, which separates it from what follows.

Responsive spacings

By default the spacing is one --femto-spacing at every width. The css/options/responsive-spacings.css option grows it at each breakpoint, from 1.25 to 2.25 times --femto-spacing, for the page's header, main and footer and for sections. Cards (<article>) grow by the same steps, vertically and horizontally. This site loads it.

Viewport width Spacing with the option
<576px 1 × --femto-spacing
≥576px 1.25 × --femto-spacing
≥768px 1.5 × --femto-spacing
≥1024px 1.75 × --femto-spacing
≥1280px 2 × --femto-spacing
≥1536px 2.25 × --femto-spacing

Load it right after femto and before your own stylesheets, like every option.
